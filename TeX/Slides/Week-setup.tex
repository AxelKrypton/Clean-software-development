\begin{frame}{A word about programming languages}
    \begin{varblock}{alert}[0.95\textwidth]{How can we work and learn together if we use different languages?}
        \begin{itemize}
            \item Most principles are language independent
            \item All used language in science are somewhat similar
            \item Trust your colleagues and focus on ideas, not technicalities
        \end{itemize}
    \end{varblock}
    \begin{varblock}{quote}[\textwidth]{The Pragmatic Programmer}[A.~Hunt \& D.~Thomas]
        «Learn at least one new language every year.
        Different languages solve the same problems in different ways.
        By learning several different approaches, you can help broaden your thinking and avoid getting struck in a rut.»
    \end{varblock}
    \begin{center}
        \Large
        \PS{Be excited, never frustrated!}
