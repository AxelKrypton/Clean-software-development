\begin{frame}[fragile]{Ex: Are these data consistent?}
\begin{varblock}{example}[\textwidth]{Ex: Are these data consistent?}
Hard to tell looking at raw data. Much easier to see with plots.
In Python, \texttt{matplotlib} naturally integrates with your scripts,
but use whatever you are comfortable with, e.g. \texttt{gnuplot}.
\begin{lstlisting}[style=mypython]

# Custom wrappers for common matplotlib tasks
ax1, ax2 = getTwinAxes()
plot_lines(t,speed,marker=None,color='red' ,label='$v$',ax=ax1)
plot_lines(t,accel,marker=None,color='blue',label='$a$',ax=ax2)

# Save people effort decoding your graph by labelling axes
set_params(xlabel='$t$ [s]',ylabel='[m/s]'    ,ax=ax1)
set_params(                 ylabel='[m/s$^2$]',ax=ax2)

plt.show()
\end{lstlisting}
\end{varblock}
